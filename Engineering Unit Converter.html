Length, area, volume, mass, pressure, energy, power, speed and temperature. Enter once, read every unit.

Every factor here is a definition

The international foot is exactly 0.3048 m, the pound exactly 0.45359237 kg, the calorie exactly 4.184 J. Derived units are stored as the product that defines them: a psi is (0.45359237 × 9.80665) ÷ 0.0254², not a rounded 6894.76, so chained conversions do not drift.

Temperature is not a ratio. It is converted through kelvin, because scales have offsets as well as different sizes.

Horsepower is two units. Mechanical hp is 550 ft·lbf/s (≈745.7 W); metric hp (PS) is 735.49875 W.

Tola = 11.6638038 g, used for gold pricing in Nepal.
